Distributional vs. Lexical Overlap (Dong et al., 2025) Cosine 0.906 vs. Jaccard 0.325 AI captures broad topic structure but substitutes flattened modern words (e.g., “gentleman”) for culturally dense concepts (junzi, “man of complete virtue”).
Thematic Cohesion & Affective Intensity (Dong et al., 2025) AI: 7 fragmented topics vs. Human: 2 core topics General LLMs smooth probability toward conservative phrasing, causing affective intensity decay and blurred moral/social distinctions.
Refusal Formula Structure (Yan & Bidin, 2025) Chinese: 4.2 strategies vs. Malaysian: 3.8 strategies Chinese learners rely heavily on Postponement + Alternative + Gratitude (16.10%); Malaysian learners pair Regret/Apology (19.38%) with controlled peer directness.
